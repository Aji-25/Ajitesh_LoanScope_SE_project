Clarifi Test Lending Policy
Version 1.0
Jurisdiction: India

Section KYC-1 Identity Verification
Every personal-loan applicant must complete identity verification before a loan decision is issued. PAN and government-issued identity evidence must be validated.

Section CONSENT-1 Applicant Consent
Explicit applicant consent must be collected before personal or financial information is stored or processed for underwriting.

Section SECURITY-1 Document Protection
Uploaded PAN, identity, and income documents must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Access must be limited to authorized underwriting personnel and recorded in an audit log.

Section UW-1 Manual Underwriting
Applications above INR 500,000 require documented manual underwriting approval before final loan approval.

Section RETENTION-1 Data Retention
Applicant documents must be retained only for the approved retention period and securely deleted when the period expires.
